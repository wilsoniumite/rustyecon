**The trap run** (C1N `p[mach]*0.5`, A1 and A2). The first parting above 1e-12 is at tick 41
(the desks' outputs, 3.9e-13 absolute); it grows to 1.6e-7 in labour's S at tick 78, where the

- A1's condition 1 (`e0/onestep.out`): the mirror's one step from the engine's own state agrees
  within 2.3e-15 at every tick from 2 to 284. The engine is the mirror's map there too.
- A2's condition 2 (`e0/pre_parting.out`): before tick 41 the largest gaps are 1.2e-14 in a price
  and 2.9e-14 in a coin, both within 1e-13 (and 9.6e-13 in labour's S and the outputs, which the
  trap amplifies on the ticks before).
- A1's condition 3 (`e0/negctl-runaway.out`): DIVERGED, shelter's price out of the runaway bound
  at tick 284 (1.014e6 of genesis), as the registered mirror's (`negctl.jsonl`: runaway at t = 284).
- Reported (A2): the mirror against itself with its genesis wage moved by 1e-15, 3e-15, 1e-14 and
  3e-14 parts by 4.7e-8, 2.2e-14, 2.8e-7 and 8.4e-8. A coin moved one ulp parts it by 5.7e-12.

Why the trap run amplifies: as the workers stop working, the wage nears the exit's money value
p_food·s₀, and F = ln1p((w − e)/(P_s + e))/χ_max is a small difference of large numbers, so a few
1e-15 in a price move the hours by 1e-12 and more; the inflation that follows (every one-sided
price stepping up, SPEC §6.8) carries it.

- The engine writes the tape (`horses tape RUN --inst ID`) and runs it (`horses run RUN --inst ID

- The mirror starts from that tape's genesis: the prices, 1 − the good desk's share, every coin,
  the one-tick stocks, the heads, the maker's finished heads and every plant.

  after the tick, the maker's markup and whether it withheld, the tasks' horse-days and labour
  supply's reach z.

**The columns.**
- "worst": the largest gap over the observables.
- "first above 1e-12": the tick, observable, engine value and mirror value.
- "but volumes": the largest gap over every observable but the cleared volumes.
- "volumes, absolute": the largest |engine − mirror| of a cleared volume over its oracle volume.
- "coins": the largest gap of a coin after the tick.
- "the mirror, one ulp": the mirror against itself with one coin, heads, finished stock or plant
  moved an ulp at genesis (HORSES-RULES §6.4's column).
- "the mirror, an ulp a tick": the same, with the ulp added after every tick. The two sides round
  apart every tick (the horse stock's (H + m) − δ·H against (1 − δ)·H + m, the plant's
  P + I′ − u·P against (1 − u)·P + I′), so this is the fairer measure of the rounding spread.

The output is `D:/rustyecon-p2b/e0/tracediff.out` (sha256 `2e7865b1…3950`).

## 3. The partings: the horse market's cancellation

Six runs part above 1e-12, each first on `vol.horse` (the four E0 runs above, and E1's two
fixed-plant runs, §4). The market clears its short side:
- the capacity desk's order δ·K\* + s_K·(K\* − H), when the stock is above its target;
- or the maker's offer, its finished stock less its cover band (F − band), when it is short.

Either is a small difference of larger terms. The script measures, at each parted tick:
- the cancellation, (the terms' sum)/(the difference), for the short side;
- the inputs' gaps: K\* and H for the order, F and the maker's plan q for the offer.

| run | parted ticks | short side | cancellation | largest gap / (cancellation × inputs' gap) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LB1 r × 2 | 4, from 198 | offer, order | 8.7–106 | 2.9 |
| LB1 b × 2 at genesis | 2, from 140 | offer | 19–41 | 6.4 |
| LB1 heads × 2 | 7, from 225 | offer, order | 10–507 | 2.0 |
| LB1 heads × 10 | 45, from 1250 | offer, order | 2.9–280 | 2.2 |
| LB1 hold, fixed plants | 2, from 468 | offer | 42–92 | 1.6 |
| LB1 w × 2, fixed plants | 2, from 712 | offer | 31–66 | 6.4 |

Every parting is its inputs' rounding gaps (at most 1.6e-13) magnified by the cancellation,
within a factor of 6.4. In absolute terms every volume agrees within 4.0e-12 of its oracle
volume. The mirror with an ulp each tick parts as far or farther in every run (1.3e-11 to
1.6e-10). This is HORSES-RULES §6.4's cancellation: its text names the maker's finished stock
among the rounding sources, as well as the stock's law of motion.

**What the volume feeds.**
- At LB1 heads × 2 and heads × 10 the maker's coin is small: 1.5e-5 and 3.0e-5 coin at the
  parted ticks, against about 0.18 at rest. So the order's revenue is most of it. The maker's
  coin and its next output inherit the volume's gap (5.9e-12 and 1.6e-11; 4.8e-12 and 1.3e-11).
- At heads × 10 the horse price carries 1.8e-12 by tick 1417.
- Every other observable of every run stays within 1e-12.

**Reading the allowance** (decision 304). LOOP-SPEC-A1 §A1.5 allows "the order's cancellation
(HORSES-RULES §6.4)". This file reads that as the horse market's cleared volume on either side,
the order or the offer. Read on the demand side alone, three runs part on the supply side, by the
same mechanism: LB1 b × 2 at 3.9e-12, and the two fixed-plant runs at 1.7e-12 and 2.4e-12. No
class or score depends on this reading.

## 7. The carry in the gate

`loops_carry_meets_the_mirror_at_tick_one` checks tick 1 against lm_carry within 1e-12, at three
runs where a planted desk's carried input binds its bundles at tick 1:
- LB1 p[traction] × 0.5: the capacity desk and the maker run on carried fodder;
- LB1 p[fodder] × 0.5: the fodder desk runs on carried horse-days;
- LW1 p[traction] × 0.5: the horse-day desk runs on carried fodder.

It checks what each carries into tick 1, and tick 1's outputs, fills and coins. Without the carry
(the registered `lm_mirror.py`) those desks make 6.4%, 6.4%, 4.7% and 3.2% less at tick 1. The
literals are `D:/rustyecon-p2b/e0/carry_literals.py`'s (output `carry_literals.out`; the search
over 34 displacements is `carry_search.py`).

A core mutant that takes the genesis lot's extra tick away fails the test. At LB1 w × 0.5,
LOOPS-RULES §10's run, labour binds every planted desk at tick 1, so the carry does not reach
the bundles there, and the test moved.

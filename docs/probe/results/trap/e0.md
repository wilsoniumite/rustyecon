**The script.** `D:/rustyecon-p24/build-trap/e0/tracediff_p.py` (sha256 `886b4f84…9624`), after the

  and the run's dials (the mirror's `over`; every rate and the pace's share a tick are checked
  equal to the tape's before the first tick);

  every actor's coin after the tick; each desk's planned share; and the paced share after the
  tick (`part_workers` against the mirror's `F`). Apart: the rule's F\* at the tick's prices
  (`part_target` against `exit_rule`'s hours over N), the regime, T_p and r_o/r;
- the mirror is run again from each genesis coin moved one ulp.

**The runs** (SPEC §8 E0): C1P `hold`, `p[labour]*2`, `JB(2)`, `p[mach]*0.5`,
`commons=48.6@genesis` (to Commons), `commons=12.15@genesis` (Enclosed) and `part.workers*0.5`;
C1P `p[mach]*0.5` at `rate.*=0.9`; C2P `hold` and `p[labour]*2`; C1PN `p[mach]*0.5`.
